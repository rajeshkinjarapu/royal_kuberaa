# Royal Kuberaa - Business Plan (100% Distribution)

**Joining Package:** ₹1000

## 1. Direct Referral Income (₹300)
- Get ₹300 instantly for every direct referral.
- Unlimited direct referrals are allowed.

## 2. Team Level Income (₹100)
- Distributed across 10 levels above the newly joined member.
- ₹10 per level (10 Levels x ₹10 = ₹100).

## 3. Global Autopool Income (₹200)
- ₹200 from every joining is allocated to the Global Autopool.
- Uses a 4x4 Global Matrix.
- Members receive Autopool income automatically as the company grows, even without referring anyone (Non-working income).

## 4. Daily Royalty Income (₹400)
₹400 from every joining is divided into different rank pools. The accumulated fund is distributed daily at 12:01 AM (via Cron Job) to the top 10 achievers in each rank using a FIFO (First In, First Out) queue system.

- **Gold Pool (₹40):** Requires 2 Directs. Max capping: ₹60,000.
- **Platinum Pool (₹60):** Max capping: ₹1,00,000.
- **Ruby Pool (₹120):** Max capping: ₹2,00,000.
- **Crown Diamond Pool (₹180):** Max capping: ₹4,00,000.

## 5. Rebirth ID System (The Infinite Engine)
- Every income generated by a user (Direct, Level, Autopool, Royalty) is split into two wallets:
  - **80%** goes to **Main Wallet** (Available for Withdrawal or P2P).
  - **20%** goes to **Rebirth Wallet** (Locked for Rebirth).
- When the Rebirth Wallet reaches ₹1000, a new **Rebirth ID** is automatically generated in the global system.
- This ensures that the Global Autopool never stops and continues to flow infinitely.
- **Note:** Rebirth IDs are only eligible for Autopool Income. They do not receive Direct, Level, or Royalty bonuses.

## Rules & Conditions
- **Minimum Withdrawal:** ₹200
- **Withdrawal Deductions:** 10% Total (5% TDS + 5% Admin Charges)
- **P2P Transfer Deductions:** 5% Charge.
- Withdrawals are processed automatically and set to 'Approved' status.

## 1. Direct Referral Income (₹400)
- Get ₹400 instantly for every direct referral.
- Unlimited direct referrals are allowed.

## 2. Team Level Income (₹300)
Instead of binary matching, you earn income up to 10 levels deep from your entire team. No matching required! The ₹300 is distributed as follows, with the highest benefits in the first 5 levels:
- **Level 1:** ₹100
- **Level 2:** ₹50
- **Level 3:** ₹40
- **Level 4:** ₹30
- **Level 5:** ₹20
- **Level 6:** ₹15
- **Level 7:** ₹15
- **Level 8:** ₹10
- **Level 9:** ₹10
- **Level 10:** ₹10

*(Note: For your direct referrals, you will receive both Direct Referral Income (₹400) + Level 1 Income (₹100) = ₹500 total!)*

## 3. Daily Royalty Pools (₹300 Fund)
Instead of a slow matrix, we use **Daily Profit Sharing**. The ₹300 from every joining is split into 4 Global Pools:
- **Gold Pool:** ₹120 (40%)
- **Platinum Pool:** ₹60 (20%)
- **Ruby Pool:** ₹60 (20%)
- **Diamond Pool:** ₹60 (20%)

At midnight (12:00 AM), the funds collected that day are distributed **EQUALLY** among all qualified members in each pool.

**Qualifications & Capping (Limits):**
- **Gold Pool:** Refer ANY 2 Directs. (You get daily share until you earn **₹20,000** total).
- **Platinum Pool:** 2 of your Directs must become Gold. (You get daily share until you earn **₹1,00,000** total).
- **Ruby Pool:** 5 of your Directs must become Platinum. (You get daily share until you earn **₹5,00,000** total).
- **Diamond Pool:** 5 of your Directs must become Ruby. (You get daily share until you earn **₹25,00,000** total).

## 4. Global FIFO Autopool / Non-Working Fund (₹150 Fund)
This is a revolutionary 100% cashback guarantee for every member who joins Royal Kuberaa!
- ₹150 from every joining package is added to the Global FIFO Autopool Fund (₹100 base + ₹50 contributed from Company Profit).
- **FIFO Distribution (First-In, First-Out):**
  - This ₹150 is distributed **EQUALLY** among the **Top 10 active members** currently in the FIFO queue (i.e. ₹15 per member from every single joining in the company!).
  - If there are fewer than 10 members in the active queue initially, the ₹150 is split equally among the available active members (`₹150 ÷ activeCount`).
- **₹1,500 100% Cashback Capping:**
  - Each member in the active Top 10 queue continues to receive their ₹15 share on every new joining until they reach **₹1,500** (recovering 100% of their joining package!).
  - Once a member completes ₹1,500, they graduate/exit the active queue with `Completed` status.
  - The next member in line (Rank #11) immediately moves into the active Top 10 queue and begins receiving ₹15 per joining.
- **Rebirth Split:**
  - Like all platform earnings, this income splits 90% into Main Wallet (withdrawable) and 10% into Rebirth Wallet.

## 5. Rebirth ID System (90% / 10% Split)
- Every income generated by a user is split into two wallets:
  - **90%** goes to **Main Wallet** (Available for Withdrawal or P2P).
  - **10%** goes to **Rebirth Wallet** (Locked for Rebirth).
- When the Rebirth Wallet reaches ₹1500, a new **Rebirth ID** is automatically generated.
- **Rebirth ID Distribution (₹1500):**
  - **₹400** goes to the Direct Sponsor (Passive Sponsor Income).
  - **₹1100** goes into the Daily Royalty Pools (Gold-₹440, Platinum-₹220, Ruby-₹220, Diamond-₹220).
  - *(Note: Rebirth IDs DO NOT generate Binary Matching, and there is no direct company profit from the rebirth allocation itself).*


## 6. Rules & Conditions
- **Minimum Withdrawal:** ₹500
- **Withdrawal Deductions:** 10% Total (5% TDS + 5% Admin Charges)
- **P2P Transfer Deductions:** 5% Charge.
- Withdrawals are processed automatically and set to 'Approved' status.
